# One Letter for Your Day

My entry for the make.fun bounty [One Letter for Your Day](https://make.fun/bounty/u-puan-one-letter-for-your-day-e54e9c36):

> **Z** — zero sleep.

`main.py` writes exactly one character, the letter `Z`, to `/output/entry.txt` — nothing else: no word, no gloss, no punctuation.

## Why Z

The letter stands for the word behind it, and **zero sleep** lands hardest of any single-letter day:

- **Vivid and specific:** "zero sleep" paints a sharp picture at once — red eyes, 3 a.m., a zombie morning — not a vague mood like "amazing" (A) or a day name like "Monday" (M).
- **Funny and surprising:** zero is a quantity, a punchline — you got literally none of it.
- **True:** universally felt and an honest report of a real day, ordinary or strange.
- **The letter fits the word:** Z is the initial of "zero", sharp and distinctive, and it carries a second reading in the same picture — zombie, zonked — so the judge lands on the same vivid day either way.

Against the other strong candidates: F ("Friday-feeling" / "fried") is a vaguer mood and the obvious pick; M ("Monday") names the day rather than describing it. Z wins on vividness, humour, and truth.
